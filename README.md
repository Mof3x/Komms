KOMMS – Restore the Signal

Premise
You are part of a network of vigilante technicianss operating from community radio station in a Sahelian town. 
A storm has damaged the transmitter; you must collect three replacement components and restore the signal before the battery runs out.

Controls

Move: WASD

Look: Mouse

Jump: Space

Sprint (if enabled): Left Shift

Interact (read notes / collect parts / repair): E

Pause: Esc

Objective and gameplay

Start in the radio station interior. Read the objective on screen.

Leave the station through the main doorway.

Explore the exterior area:

Follow the paths to find three transmitter components.

Look at components or community notes until an “Press E to interact” prompt appears, then press E.

Avoid the patrol scanner / hazard area or move quickly through it.

Once you have all components (HUD counter shows 3/3), return to the transmitter inside the station.

Press E on the transmitter to repair it and see the CONNECTION RESTORED end screen.

If you pause the game, you can adjust a master volume slider in the settings/pause menu and then resume play.
